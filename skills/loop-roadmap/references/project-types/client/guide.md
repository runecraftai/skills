# Interview Guide: client (rigor: standard)

## Section: problem             # required
Explore:
- Who is the client, and what does their business need this site to do?
- What do they use today (spreadsheet, nothing, another tool)?
Suggest:
- The problem is what the *client's customer* can't do today, not what the client wants to build.
- If the client hasn't articulated a user need: "walk me through your customer's current experience."

## Section: audience            # required
Explore:
- Who visits this site, and what are they trying to accomplish?
- Is the audience new visitors (discovery) or returning users (repeat action)?
Suggest:
- One primary audience with one primary action is easier to ship than "everyone."
- Frame audience in terms of intent: "someone who wants to X" rather than demographics.

## Section: conversion-goal     # required
Explore:
- What action means success for this site? (form fill, purchase, call, signup?)
- How will you measure it?
Suggest:
- Pick one conversion event as the north star; secondary goals are phase 2.
- If measurement is fuzzy: start with a count (form submissions/week) — instrumentation is its own phase.

## Section: out-of-scope        # required (scope guardrail)
Explore:
- What are you explicitly NOT building in v1?
Suggest:
- Admin dashboards, CMS backends, and analytics panels are phase 2 unless they're the product.
- Content strategy and SEO tooling are separate disciplines — flag them as out of scope.
