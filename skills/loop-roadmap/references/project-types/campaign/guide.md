# Interview Guide: campaign (rigor: creative)

## Section: problem             # required
Explore:
- What gap does this campaign fill? (awareness, conversion, retention, launch?)
- What does the audience currently believe or do that needs to change?
Suggest:
- Anchor to a specific audience behavior shift, not a content format.
- If the problem is "we need more content": reframe to "who needs to know what."

## Section: goal                # required
Explore:
- What number moves by when? (signups, traffic, revenue, attendance?)
- Is there a single event or deadline anchoring this?
Suggest:
- One measurable goal; secondary metrics are tracking, not targets.
- If the goal is vague ("grow the brand"): ask "how would you know in 30 days if it worked?"

## Section: timeline            # required
Explore:
- When does this campaign start, and when must it deliver?
- What are the checkpoints where you evaluate whether to keep going?
Suggest:
- Map phases to calendar weeks; a campaign without a stop date accumulates scope.
- Build in a "kill decision" checkpoint where you decide to double down or stop.

## Section: out-of-scope        # required (scope guardrail)
Explore:
- What tactics, channels, or asset types are explicitly NOT part of this campaign?
Suggest:
- Channels you're not active on, formats you're not producing, audiences you're not targeting.
- "We'll do everything" is the fastest way to do nothing — constrain to one channel first.
